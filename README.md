  <h2>Project Overview</h2>

  <p>
    This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using the Brazilian Olist
    e-commerce dataset. The goal was to understand revenue performance, repeat
    purchasing, customer segments, cohort retention, and churn risk.
  </p>

  <p>
    The analysis transforms raw transaction data into an interactive Power BI
    dashboard supported by PostgreSQL analytical views and Python validation notebooks.
  </p>

  <h2>Important Findings</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>
      Revenue increased from approximately R$40K in 2016 to R$7.22M in 2018.
    </li>
    <li>
      November 2017 was the strongest month, generating approximately R$987.8K.
    </li>
    <li>
      São Paulo was the leading revenue-generating state.
    </li>
    <li>
      Only 2.91% of the customer population were identified as repeat buyers.
    </li>
    <li>
      The At Risk segment generated approximately R$4.71M from 22,033 customers.
    </li>
    <li>
      Potential Customers were the largest RFM segment, with 33,776 customers.
    </li>
    <li>
      The churn model classified 55,381 customers as churned using a 180-day inactivity rule.
    </li>
    <li>
      Cohort retention generally decreased as the number of months after the first purchase increased.
    </li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Business Recommendations</h2>

  <ol>
    <li>
      Improve first-to-second purchase conversion through personalized follow-up
      campaigns and product recommendations.
    </li>
    <li>
      Prioritize At Risk and High Value At Risk customers with targeted win-back campaigns.
    </li>
    <li>
      Build retention strategies for high-revenue states, beginning with São Paulo.
    </li>
    <li>
      Track movement from Potential Customers into Loyal Customers and Champions.
    </li>
    <li>
      Compare customer retention by product category before making investment decisions.
    </li>
  </ol>

  <h2>Project Outcome</h2>

  <p>
    The project demonstrates how SQL, Python, and Power BI can be combined to move
    from raw e-commerce data to practical business decisions. The main opportunity
    identified is improving customer retention and converting more first-time buyers
    into repeat customers.
  </p>
